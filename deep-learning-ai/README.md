# Prompting

### 5 Building Blocks of a Good Prompt

| # | Building Block | Description | Example | 
| - | - | - | - |
| 1 | Goal | What you want to create | create a web page to help me write birthday cards |
| 2 | Input | What the users provide | when i give a person's name, age and hobby, it should give me back a funny message |
| 3 | Layout | How the app should look | make it prettier by adding a festive title and colors |
| 4 | Features | What special features to include | display the card on the right side, and make it look like the inside of a birthday card |
| 5 | Output | What the app should output | add a fun title at the top of the page. and instead of replacing the old cards, keep them visible and stack them underneath the new ones |

### Combining building blocks
1. Start with your __goal__
1. There are multiple ways to put the blocks together
1. You don't have to use all the blocks
1. The order is not very important
1. AI is pretty good at understanding the different rearragements of the building blocks

### Additional Notes
1. You can prompt step by step, iterate over multiple times to improve the result
1. You can also use a single long detailed prompt if you know all the specifications up front to generate a better first version, which you can also still continue to iterate and improve on
1. Results may vary even with specific prompts

### Get Some Feedback

Feedback is a great way to improve software apps:
1. Show it to a friend
1. Show it to your family
1. Respectfully approach strangers
1. Email it to a colleague
1. Post it on a forum

### Think about ordering food

| Prompt | Output |
| - | - |
| give me a sandwich | gives you any kind of sandwich |
| i would like a vegetarian sandwich | gives you vegetables and eliminates meats and other stuffs |
| i would like a vegetarian sandwich with hummus and cheese on multigrain bread | gives you the exact ingredients that you are looking for |
| i would like a vegetarian sandwich with hummus and cheese on multigran bread. add a drink and make it to-go, please! | becomes much more predictable what AI gives you back |

Being __specific__ with your instructions to AI will give better results.

### Add New Features To Your App

Add new features:
1. Five input fields
1. "I'm feeling lucky" button
1. Update the title
1. Button to copy message to clipboard
1. Change the color theme

Make it yours:
1. Make it have five clearly labelled inputs: name, age, hobby, adjective, plural noun
1. add an "i'm feeling lucky" button that will automatically fill in the blanks with random words. give it some funny pre-defined word choices
1. make the titles and subtitles fancier. add buttons to copy the cards to the clipboard
1. make the color theme blue

You can always change your mind:
1. i don't like the blue color theme, make it purple instead

### Troubleshooting

1. nothing happens when i click on the "generate card" button. can you fix it for me?

### When Crafting Your Own Prompts

1. Be specific to get better results
1. Use the building blocks as a toolkit to draw from as needed
1. Use your chatbot to iterate, improve and troubleshoot
